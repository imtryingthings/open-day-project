Open Day Project

Travel & Carbon Footprint Survey

This project explores how students and visitors travel to school and how much carbon dioxide they produce. The results help us understand travel habits, compare transport choices, and think about more sustainable ways to move.

Walking Zero emissions
Public Transport Lower carbon impact
Private Car Higher emissions
EV Lower than petrol/diesel

Project aim

  • Measure how people travel to school.
  • Compare transport choices and emissions.
  • Encourage more sustainable everyday travel habits.
  • Collect anonymous data for analysis and discussion.
